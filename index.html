About Me

Who I Am

I'm Mohd Farhan Raza, a third-year B.Tech student in Computer Science & Engineering at United College of Engineering and Research, Greater Noida. I got into web development through sheer curiosity — started with a YouTube tutorial, broke something, fixed it, and kind of never stopped.

During my two months at CodeAlpha as a frontend intern, I shipped three production-ready applications. The biggest thing I took away wasn't a specific skill — it was learning how to write code that someone else can actually read and work with.

Outside of the internship, I've built four projects end-to-end on my own — hotel booking, an e-commerce store, a student records system, and a food ordering app. Each one was an attempt to solve something real, even if it was just "how would this actually work if real users were using it?"

Currently learning Node.js and MongoDB to round out my backend knowledge. Long-term goal is to build things that actually matter at a product-focused tech company.
